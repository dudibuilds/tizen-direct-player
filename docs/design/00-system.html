Design system proposal

Blue-black ink, artwork doing the colour, white for where you are, one warm amber for what is yours: your progress, your selection, the one thing to press. Sized for 3 m from a 55" panel; every component below is drawn at its real 1920×1080 size. The wordmark and the name "direct" are placeholders.

Motion

Two curves. Things that travel or scale use cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1): quick off the mark, long soft landing. Things that appear or leave fade: ease-out in, ease-in out, and out is always shorter than in. Order: focus first, content second, atmosphere last. Only position, scale and opacity change. Demos loop every 4 s.
